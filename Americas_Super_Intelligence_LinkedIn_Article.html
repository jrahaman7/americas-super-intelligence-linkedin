1. The name change

The order is titled Inaugurating the Era of Super Intelligence. Executive-branch websites, speeches, reports, and policy memos must say Super Intelligence or SI. Agencies will not acknowledge the words artificial intelligence or AI in non-statutory settings. For now SI means whatever 15 U.S.C. 9401(3) already calls AI. OSTP has 60 days to propose a federal definition that may expand or replace that statute.

2. One search bar for the Republic

America.gov is operated by GSA. No login in Phase 1. Launch officials said the models include Google Gemini and xAI Grok. It searches about 29,000 federal sites. Answers cite official pages. Upload a PDF and personal data is stripped in the browser. English, Spanish, French. Voice and file drop.
